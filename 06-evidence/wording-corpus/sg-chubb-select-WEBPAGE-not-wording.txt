 SME Package Policy (Select+) 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Chubb understands that SMEs are usually more vulnerable to fluctuating economic conditions and business interruptions due to smaller cash flows. We have designed Select+®, a package policy for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), to offer wide standard cover with optional add-ons. Select+ provides SMEs with the protection they need, according to their requirements. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 

 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 


 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Key Features of Select+ 
 
 
 
 Wide Standard Cover 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Property 
 
 
 First-party property damage 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Money 
 
 
 Theft of cash within premises or whilst in transit 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Fidelity Guarantee 
 
 
 Employee dishonesty 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Public Liability / General Liability 
 
 
 Third party bodily injury or property damage 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Workers Injury Compensation 
 
 
 Statutory cover for employees against work-related injuries 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Goods In Transit 
 
 
 Movement of goods anywhere within Singapore 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Specified All Risks for Portable Items 
 
 
 Laptops, mobile phones and other specified items for damage anywhere in Singapore or worldwide 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Business Interruption 
 
 
 Protection against loss of profits in the event of damage to your business 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Machinery Breakdown 
 
 
 Physical machinery breakdown 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Group Personal Accident 
 
 
 Personal injury or death to insured employees 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 



 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Industries Focus 
 
 
 
 Coverage Highlight for each Target Industry 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Food & Beverages (F&B) 
 
 
 Restaurants, dining places, food courts, food court stalls, coffee or ice cream shops and canteen operations.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Retailers 
 
 
 Grocery stores and retailers of all types of goods in Singapore including but not limited to pharmacies, electronics, cosmetics, fashion, clothing, liquor, toys games and furniture. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Education 
 
 
 Pre-school education centres, tuition centres, commercial schools, children enrichment classes, music dance and ballet schools, art or fashion schools, adult education centres and language schools. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Health, Beauty and Spa 
 
 
 Beauty and hair salons, spa and foot reflexology clinics, weight loss and slimming centres, manicure and pedicure parlours, makeover services and more. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Hospitality & Hotels 
 
 
 Fitness centres, gymnasiums, hotels, hostels, motel and more. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Medical & Dental Centres 
 
 
 Psychiatry and general practice medical clinics, chiropractic and acupuncture services, specialist medical centres, aesthetics and cosmetic services, dental services and more. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Office & Services Business 
 
 
 Management consultants, accountants and IT consultants, software designers, architects, estate and letting agents, financial advisers, insurance brokers and agents, health and safety consultants and marketing companies.
