# VERIS — Pitch Deck Prompts

20 slides. Copy one fenced block, paste into your image generator, get a finished slide. Nothing to assemble.

Slides **1–20** are the deck. A1–A3 are optional backup, only worth building for live judging.

**Slides 8 and 9 answer the problem statement clause by clause** — 8 is the diagnosis half, 9 is the "guide them at every stage" half plus the in-product navigator.

Video uses slides **1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 19, 20** — hold 20 silent for the last three seconds. If you want the PS mapping in the video, add slide 8 for ~15 seconds; that takes you to roughly 115 seconds, still inside the 2-minute ceiling.
